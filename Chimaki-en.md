# Chimaki Privacy Policy

Chimaki does not collect any data other than user settings preferences, and will not use any of them for advertisement or analytical use.

Translation will be handled by the model you've chosen. Apple Translate, Apple Intelligence and Private Cloud Compute all follow Apple's privacy policies.

Models marked as "local" will be expected to work locally, but cannot ensure that your data will not be transmitted to Apple or the third-party service provider you've chosen through the internet.

This document may change without prior notifications.

The Simplified Chinese of this privacy policy should be the final reference in case meanings differ between translations.
